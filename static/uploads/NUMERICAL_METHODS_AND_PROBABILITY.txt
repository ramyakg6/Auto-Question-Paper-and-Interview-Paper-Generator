NUMERICAL METHODS AND PROBABILITY
Course Code: 24CSK42 
Subject: Mathematics - Common to AIML, CSE and CDS

MODULE-1: INTRODUCTION TO JAVA (8 Hours)
-The Java Language, Java Development Kit (JDK)
-Java Buzzwords, Byte Code, JVM, JRE and Java environment 
-Data types, variables and Arrays
-Operators, Control statement
-command line Arguments
-Object Oriented concepts, Classes, Objects and Methods, Access specifiers, Method Overloading, Constructor, Implicit this. 

MODULE-2: INHERITANCE AND INTERFACING (8 Hours)
-Inheritance, Method Overriding
-Annotations
-Static members, Inner Classes
-Abstract Classes, Final members and classes
-The Object Class
-Interfaces, Package Fundamentals
-Reflections 

MODULE-3: VECTOR SPACES (8 Hours)
- Vector Space definition and examples
- Subspaces and Spanning sets
- Linear Dependence and Independence
- Linear Independence and Spanning Sets
- Bases: Orthogonal and Orthonormal bases and Dimension

MODULE-4: PROBABILITY AND PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTIONS (8 Hours)
- Random variables (discrete and continuous)
- Probability density functions
- Expectation and Standard Deviation
- Discrete Probability distributions: Binomial and Poisson Distributions
- Problems on Probability distributions
- Continuous Probability distribution: Normal Distributions
- Concept of joint probability
- Joint probability distribution
- Dependent and Independent random variables
- Covariance and Correlation coefficient

MODULE-5: SAMPLING THEORY (8 Hours)
- Sampling and Sampling distributions
- Test of hypothesis of large samples for means and proportions
- Inferences for variance and proportion
- Central limit theorem (without proof)
- Confidence limits for means
- Student's t-distribution
- F-distribution
- Chi-square distribution for test of goodness of fit for small samples

Bloom's Taxonomy Mapping:
L1 - Remember: Recall definitions, formulae, and basic concepts
L2 - Understand: Explain methods and interpret results
L3 - Apply: Solve numerical problems using methods
L4 - Analyze: Compare methods, analyze solutions
L5 - Evaluate: Justify choice of method, assess accuracy
L6 - Create: Design solutions, formulate new problems

Course Code: 24MAC31

MODULE-1: NUMERICAL SOLUTIONS AND INTERPOLATION (8 Hours)
Numerical solution of algebraic and transcendental equations:
- Newton-Raphson Method
- Problems on interpolation
- Newton's forward and backward formulae for equal intervals
- Newton divided difference
- Lagrange's formula for unequal intervals (without proofs)
- Problems on interpolation

Key Topics:
- Use Newton's forward formula for equal interval problems
- Use Newton's backward formula for equal interval problems
- Uses of Simpson's 1/3rd rule problems
- Uses of Simpson's 3/8th rule problems
- Use Wronskian to test a set of solutions of a linear homogeneous differential equation for linear independence

MODULE-2: NUMERICAL SOLUTIONS TO DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS AND INTEGRATION (8 Hours)
Numerical solution of ordinary differential equations of first order and first degree:
- Taylor's series method
- Modified Euler's method
- Runge-Kutta method of fourth-order problems
- Milne's predictor and corrector methods
- Numerical integration: Simpson's 1/3rd rule, Simpson's 3/8th rule, Weddle's rule (without proofs)
- Application of numerical integration to velocity of a particle and volume of solids

Key Topics:
- Identify and sketch the graph of a conic section and perform a rotation of axes
- Use of Binomial Distribution in real life problems
- Use of Normal Distribution in real life problems
- Use Student's t-distribution to test goodness of fit for small samples
- Use Chi-square distribution to test goodness of fit for small samples
